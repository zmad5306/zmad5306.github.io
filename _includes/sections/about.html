I’m a software engineer and systems architect with nearly two decades of experience building and modernizing complex systems across government and private-sector organizations.

My work spans generations of technology — from mainframe systems to cloud-native platforms — but the focus has always been the same: designing systems that are reliable, scalable, and built to last. I care about clear boundaries, strong data models, and architectures that hold up under real-world pressure.

I work across languages and platforms as needed — including C#, Java, and JavaScript — but I don’t define myself by a stack. The problem space drives the tools, not the other way around.

I treat AI as a force multiplier, not a replacement for engineers. It’s a capable junior developer: fast and tireless, but still in need of direction. I use it to accelerate execution while I focus on architecture, intent, and correctness. Writing code isn’t the job — engineering reliable systems is.
